### 0.

IgCaller consists of an algorithm written in Python language designed to characterize immunoglobulin (IG) B-cell receptor and T-cell receptor (TCR) gene rearrangements and oncogenic translocations in lymphoid neoplasms from next generation sequencing (NGS) data. It can work with NGS data obtained from whole-genome sequencing, whole-exome sequencing, and gene panels. IgCaller reports the IG and TCR gene rearrangements identified together with the annotation of the genes and alleles involved and their somatic hypermutation status. IgCaller also reports specific IG features such as chronic lymphocytic leukemia stereotype subsets, IGLV3-21 R110 mutation, and acquired N-glycosylation sites. Each identified rearrangement is reported together with metrics of quality control and support evidence.
